## 🌸 What is Pip?

**Pip** is a tiny pomodoro companion with a big heart. Pick a pet, start a focus session, and watch it grow while soft lofi beats play in the background. A floating mini-timer stays on top of your other windows — so you never have to leave your work to check the clock.

Everything runs **100% locally** in your browser. No backend, no sign-ups, no servers. Your focus time, your pet, and your stats live only on your device.

Built by [**Alisa**](https://t.me/aliskamalyshkaa) — a 20-year-old who loves cute things, cozy vibes, and tools that respect your privacy. Meet **Pip** and friends, the chibi companions who focus alongside you. 🌱

## ✨ Features

### ⏱️ Pomodoro timer
- Classic **Focus / Short break / Long break** cycles
- Adjustable durations (default: 25 / 5 / 15 minutes)
- Warning color + blinking on the last minute and last 10 seconds
- Hotkeys — `Space` to start, `R` to reset

### 🎵 Procedural lofi music
- **Never-repeating tracks** — every session sounds different
- Random key, chord progression, tempo (68–90 BPM), and length
- Six moods: **Jazzy, Dreamy, Mellow, Nostalgic, Rainy, Cosmic**
- Moods don't repeat within the last 3 tracks
- Warm pads, bass, lo-fi drums, and vinyl crackle
- Volume slider in settings

### 📺 Picture-in-Picture mini timer
- Floating timer that **stays on top of every window** — Chrome, VS Code, Photoshop, anything
- Works outside the browser on desktop
- Fully synced with the main timer — start, pause, reset from the PiP window
- Themed to match your pet
- *Requires Chrome 116+ or Edge 116+*

### 🏆 Growth & achievements
- **8 stages**: Seed → Sprout → Baby → Tiny → Teen → Adult → Sage → Legend
- **19 achievements** across sessions, focus hours, streaks, stages, and daily goals
- Unlock notifications with sound and animation
- Dedicated achievements modal

### 🔥 Streaks & goals
- Daily focus goal with a progress bar
- Streak counter with best-streak tracking
- Heat-themed badge when you're on a roll 🔥

### 🎁 Nice extras
- **Dark, soft, glassy interface** with per-pet color theming
- Fully **responsive** — works on mobile and desktop
- Progress saved locally (`localStorage`) — no backend needed
- Gentle UI sound effects (mutable)
- **Squash & stretch** physics when you drag your pet

## 🛡️ Privacy first

Unlike most productivity apps, Pip **never sends your data anywhere**:

| What other services do | What Pip does |
|---|---|
| Require an account | No account, no email, no tracking |
| Sync your data to a cloud | Everything stays in **your browser** |
| Show ads | No ads, ever |
| Sell your focus stats | Nothing is collected |
| Lock features behind a paywall | All features are free and open-source |

Close the tab — and your progress is still there when you come back. Because it never left your device.

## 🧪 Honest limitations

We love transparency, so here's what to know before you use it:

- **Picture-in-Picture mini timer only works in Chrome 116+ and Edge 116+.** Firefox and Safari don't support the Document PiP API yet — you'll get a friendly message instead. The main timer still works everywhere.
- **Lofi music is generated in real time.** Every track is unique, but the generator has a "mood" — sometimes two tracks in a row can feel similar. Press the music button again for a fresh one.
- **Progress is stored locally.** That means: clearing your browser data resets your pet, and stats don't sync between devices. No cloud, no backup — by design.
- **No background timers.** If you close the tab, the timer stops. Keep the PiP window open for a persistent mini-timer.
- **Sound requires a click first.** Browsers block autoplay — you have to press the music or sound button once before audio starts.

## 🛠️ Tech stack

- **Vanilla JavaScript** — no frameworks, no build tools
- **Web Audio API** — for procedural lofi music and UI sounds
- **Document Picture-in-Picture API** — for the floating mini timer
- **SVG** — every pet is drawn as vector graphics with animated parts
- **localStorage** — for saving progress locally
- **Google Fonts** — Inter typeface

The whole app is **one HTML file**. That's the point. Copy it, fork it, remix it.
